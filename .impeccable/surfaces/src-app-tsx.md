# App shell and every signed-in screen

Scope: the whole signed-in app and the intro page. Visitor mode: Operate (reading surfaces: Read). Redesign of the visual world with every layout, feature and piece of content kept (user's choice, September 2026).

Audience and job: people weighing a career move, finding who is one step ahead and asking them something specific.

Kept by the user: the Path as a transit line, the logo mark, every layout, feature and piece of content. Chosen by the user (September 29, 2026): Soft Cream #FFF7ED ground, Midnight Navy #0F172A as the main accent, and the styling and type of a reference site (Space Grotesk). Asked for: more of the intro's assembling motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: PathedIn as a calm, confident place to plan a career: warm cream paper, navy ink, and the product itself shown on Midnight Navy panels where your Path draws itself. Refuses the graphite dev-tool look it replaces and the generic white SaaS template.

OWN-WORLD: Soft Cream canvas (#FFF7ED) with warm white cards (#FFFCF8) and navy-8% hairlines; Midnight (#0F172A) as the dark appearance with cream type. Midnight Navy is the main accent: primary buttons, the walked Path, active states, and immersive panels (navy gradient with a blue glow, lit by route silk: many routes flowing to one destination, yours brighter). One signal blue (#2448C8; #8FA8FF on navy) for links, the future, focus and numeral units. Space Grotesk for interface, headings (large and regular, tight tracking) and the bold wordmark; Charter for long-form reading and quoted words. Radii 6/8 for controls, 16 for cards, 20 for sheets and panels, 28 for the intro stage; pills for tags and counts.

STORY: open PathedIn and your Path is drawn for you on navy, the people on it arrive in order, and every number counts up to where you are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: cream top bar with the bold wordmark and a ⌘K field, sidebar with a sliding active surface, the For you feed, and a right column where Coming up sits on a white card and Your Path this week is a Midnight Navy panel that draws itself. On iPhone: large title, Coming up cards, your week as a navy panel, tab bar.

FORM: canon, pinned by the user to their reference site (look) and to Linear, Arc and Raycast (motion); no roll (seed key: none, user-pinned). Signature: the intro stage and the portrait flight onto the Path. Motion grammar: things assemble once on arrival (lines rise out of a soft blur, cards rise 55 ms apart, capped; Paths and silk draw; numbers count up), feedback in 90–240 ms, screens change in 200 ms, overlays grow from 0.98; no bounce, no loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
