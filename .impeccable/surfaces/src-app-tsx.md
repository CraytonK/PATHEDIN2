## Direction contract

THESIS: PathedIn as a calm, confident place to plan a career: warm cream paper, navy ink, and the product itself shown on Midnight Navy panels where your Path draws itself. Refuses the graphite dev-tool look it replaces and the generic white SaaS template.

OWN-WORLD: Soft Cream canvas (#FFF7ED) with a fine paper grain and white cards (20px corners, a faint navy hairline and a long, soft fall-off) carrying every screen's content, and cream wells inside them; Midnight (#0F172A) as the dark appearance with cream type. Midnight Navy is the main accent: primary buttons, the walked Path, active states, and immersive panels (flat navy with a fine grain, lit by route silk: many routes flowing to one destination, yours brighter). One signal blue (#2448C8; #8FA8FF on navy) for links, the future, focus and numeral units. Space Grotesk for interface, headings (large and regular, tight tracking) and the bold wordmark; Charter for long-form reading and quoted words. Whatever is current is a sliding navy pill (top bar section, tab, phone tab, today). Radii 8 for small controls, 20 for cards, sheets and panels, 28 for the intro stage; pills for tags, counts, tab tracks and the top bar.

STORY: open PathedIn and your Path is drawn for you on navy, the people on it arrive in order, and every number counts up to where you are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a white top bar (bold wordmark, cream ⌘K field; the five sections as icons in the middle with a sliding navy pill; Write, messages, notifications and your name chip on the right). Home in three columns: your profile card (route-silk cover, counts, your Path as tags, shortcuts, Write); People worth knowing as faces in drawing rings above For you and the posts as white cards; Coming up as a week strip and schedule, Your Path this week on navy, Office Hours and your communities. On iPhone: large title, Coming up cards, your week as a navy panel, cards, and a floating tab bar with a sliding navy pill.

FORM: canon, pinned by the user to their reference site (look) and to Linear, Arc and Raycast (motion); no roll (seed key: none, user-pinned). Signature: the intro stage and the portrait flight onto the Path. Motion grammar: things assemble once on arrival (lines rise out of a soft blur, cards rise 55 ms apart, capped; Paths and silk draw; numbers count up), feedback in 90–240 ms, screens change in 200 ms, overlays grow from 0.98; no bounce, no loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
