# App shell and every signed-in screen

Scope: the whole signed-in app and the intro page. Visitor mode: Operate (reading surfaces: Read). Redesign of the visual world with every layout, feature and piece of content kept (user's choice, September 2026).

Audience and job: people weighing a career move, finding who is one step ahead and asking them something specific.

Kept by the user: the Path as a transit line, Executive Navy, the logo and wordmark. Replaced: the cream ground and the previous fonts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: PathedIn as a precise instrument for a big decision. The Path is the one bright, living line on a quiet, layered, graphite surface. Refuses the editorial cream-and-serif reading app it was, and the near-black neon-glow template.

OWN-WORLD: graphite dark by default (#0B0C0F canvas; surfaces #121418, #17191E, #1D2026; 1px borders at white 7–10% with a 4% top highlight) and a porcelain light (#F6F7F9 canvas, white surfaces, slate borders). Executive Navy fills primary actions and marks the future; lifted to #8EA2F2 for text on graphite. Mona Sans for all interface and headings (semibold, slightly wide, tight tracking); Charter only for long-form reading; the Playfair wordmark stays. Radii 6/8/12/16, pills only for tags and counts. Soft layered elevation, no glow.

STORY: open the app and it feels fast and exact. Everything answers to the Path, and one keystroke reaches anyone or anything.

FIRST VIEWPORT: compact sidebar with a sliding active surface, slim top bar with a ⌘K launcher, the For you feed as hover-lit rows, the right column as stacked layered cards; on iPhone, large title, layered cards, tab bar.

FORM: canon, pinned by the user to Linear, Arc and Raycast; no roll (seed key: none, user-pinned). Signature interaction: the ⌘K command palette, "Go anywhere on your Path". Motion grammar: 150–200 ms ease-out feedback, spring layout for shared indicators, 200 ms fade-rise between screens, overlays scale from 0.98.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
